Overview

What this product is

EliteSchool helps schools manage student achievement through verified school work. Students build a trusted record of contribution. Faculty verify effort. Elite Points measure that progress. The rewards catalog turns points into materials and opportunities so students who need support are not left behind.

Achievement first. Rewards are a support layer — not the core story.
